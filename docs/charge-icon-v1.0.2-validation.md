Two native exports produced identical output hashes. The App PNG is 1024 px sRGB/RGBA8 with transparent corners and exact opaque palette values #262425, #E9E7E4 and #C6CA4C. The parent and two independent reviewers inspected actual PNG previews at 16/18/32/64/128/256 pt and light/dark menu variants.
